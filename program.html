01 What changed

The original sheet was 169 sets a week, 28 per session, with five pulldown variations, one hinge movement and no conditioning. That is a mass program running on a deficit.

  • Weekly sets169 → 141
  • Chest direct sets27 → 19
  • Back direct sets28 → 20
  • Pulldown variations5 → 2
  • Hinge movements1 → 2
  • Calf sets6 → 10
  • Direct grip work0 → 3
  • Conditioning0 → 120-160 min
The one rule behind the cuts: sets past roughly 20 per muscle per week buy very little extra growth and cost recovery you need for the deficit. Chest, back and quads were all above that at once.

03 Three blocks

1Weeks 1-4

Normalize

RIR 3 on main lifts, clean baselines, hinge installed. Cardio 3 x 25 min zone 2, 8k steps.

Week 4: mini-deload, 60% sets, 10% lighter
2Weeks 5-8

Intensify

Top set plus back-offs on mains, RIR 1-2. Accessories rotate. Cardio 3 x 30 min plus one interval session.

Week 8: full deload, 50% sets, eat at maintenance
3Weeks 9-12

Density

Accessories move to 12-20 reps, shorter rests, supersets. Cardio 4 x 30 min if weight loss has stalled.

Week 12: test, then 5-7 easy days

05 Progression rules

One algorithm, applied to every exercise. Log the set, and the next load follows from it.

  1. Top of the range on every set, at target RIR: add 5 lb on upper-body barbell and all machines, 10 lb on lower-body barbell.
  2. Jump too big: if the increment is over 5% of the load (10% on squat, hinge and lunge patterns), hold the weight and add one rep instead. Dumbbells add 5 lb per hand, and use the rep push when the next bell is over 7% heavier.
  3. Inside the range: same load, one more rep on the last set.
  4. Missed the bottom twice: cut one set for two weeks and take 7.5% off the bar. Rebuild.
  5. Flat for three sessions: swap inside the same pattern. New baseline is a weight you can do for the bottom of the range plus two, at RIR 2.
  6. Two main lifts flat in one week: take the deload early. Fatigue is systemic, not exercise specific.
RIR by type: main barbell 1-2, secondary compounds 2, machines and dumbbells 1-2, isolation 0-1 on the last set only. RPE 10 on a main lift stays off the table until week 12.

  • Intervals: 30 sec hard, 90 sec easy, 6-8 rounds, bike or rower1x / week

That is roughly 800-1200 kcal a week, about 0.2 to 0.35 lb of fat on its own, without touching recovery the way extra lifting would.
